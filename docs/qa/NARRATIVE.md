# Narrative, world and completeness

## The direction on record

No plot (PLAN.md §2f, the user, 2026-10-31): *giants own the lands, they live here; you are a
visitor, simply curious.* The story is told by the world — words on stone, a traveller's camps and
pages, things left in high places, what the giants do — and kept in the journal. STORY.md's plot
options (the sky coming down, etc.) are superseded and stay unbuilt. This report judges the game
against that direction, not against a plot it chose not to have.
